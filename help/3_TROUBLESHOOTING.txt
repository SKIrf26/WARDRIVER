3. TROUBLESHOOTING
==================

The board prints start-up messages over USB that help a lot: open any serial terminal at 115200 baud on the port you
flashed through (the LEFT USB-C port). Every run start and stop is printed, with the log file name.

A. The computer does not see the board / flashing fails
   - Use a USB-C DATA cable. Try another USB port on the computer.
   - Use the LEFT USB-C port for flashing (the USB-UART port). The middle port needs download mode by hand: hold BOOT,
     tap RESET, release BOOT.
   - Hold the board's BOOT button while plugging the cable in, release it, then try again.
   - If you have a buzzer fitted and flashing keeps failing, unplug it while you flash (IO26 is a boot-mode pin).

B. Nothing shows on the screen, or the colours are wrong
   - Unplug the board, wait 10 seconds, plug it in again. Try another cable or USB port.
   - Flash the .bin again (help/2_FLASHING.txt).
   - Red and blue look swapped everywhere (the accent that should be green looks right, but orange looks blue):
     your board has a different panel. Build from source with SKIRF_PANEL_BGR set to 0 at the top of the sketch
     (docs/BUILDING.md), and please report it with a photo of the board's back.

C. Touch does nothing, or lands in the wrong place
   - Settings > Touch calibration, and tap each corner marker exactly (a stylus or fingernail tip helps).
   - Cannot reach Settings? Hold the BOOT button while you plug the board in: it goes straight to calibration.
     On Home you can also press and hold an empty spot for 3 seconds.
   - Changing the orientation always asks for a new calibration. That is normal.

D. It says WAITING FOR A GPS FIX (or WAITING FOR MORE SATELLITES) and never starts scanning
   A run scans only while the GPS has a fix with at least 4 satellites (5 on foot).
   - Go outdoors or put the module where it can see the sky. The first fix after power-up can take several minutes.
   - Open GPS from Home and read the FIX tile:
       SEARCH     the module is talking. While it searches, SATELLITES shows "used / in view", for example 0 / 14.
                  Satellites in view but no fix: give the antenna a clearer view of the sky and wait (a cold start
                  can take 1 to 5 minutes; a car roof or dashboard is better than inside a building). 0 in view after
                  a few minutes outdoors: the antenna is not connected, faulty, or shielded.
       NO DATA    nothing arrives on the RX pin (the line under the compass says which). Check the wiring: the
                  module's TX goes to the board's RX (IO4 by default), and power and ground must be connected. On many
                  boards IO4 receives nothing: wire the module's TX to IO8 (RX to IO9) and pick the 8 / 9 preset in
                  Settings > GPS pins, tap SAVE (the board restarts) and look again. Flashing resets the pins.
       BAD BAUD   bytes arrive but none can be read. The board tries 9600, 38400 and 115200 baud (and swapped RX/TX)
                  by itself, about every 8 seconds; if it stays on BAD BAUD, check for a loose wire or noise.
   - The Serial Monitor (115200 baud) prints a "GPS:" line every 10 seconds with the pins, baud, characters received,
     good and bad sentences, satellites used and in view. Include it when you report a GPS problem.
   - Keep the GPS antenna a little away from the board and its display (a few centimetres helps).
   - For the fastest first fix, tap SKIP on the web portal at start-up: its Wi-Fi hotspot is the noisiest thing near
     the antenna while the GPS is searching. "Wi-Fi on: slows the fix" under the heading means the radio is on:
     leave the Wi-Fi screens and it turns off after 20 seconds.
   - Position or speed frozen, or coming in bursts: under HDOP the GPS screen shows Fix % and "Moved N sec ago".
     A low Fix % means the module is losing the fix. "Moved" climbing while you walk: connect the module's RX to
     the board's TX pin so the board can set its walking mode.
   - A module that stopped talking after an older release sent it set-up commands stays silent until it loses power:
     unplug the USB cable for 10 seconds (and remove any power bank) so the module restarts.
   - In Pro mode, a strict GPS accuracy setting (HDOP 2, 6 sats) can hold back a run: set it to Off.

E. It finds few or no networks
   - Check the GPS first (D): no fix, no scanning.
   - CAR and MOTO skip the slow 5 GHz radar (DFS) channels on two sweeps out of three. WALK/BIKE scans everything
     every sweep. Pick the ride that matches how fast you are moving.
   - In Pro mode, check Wi-Fi scan / BLE scan are On, Channels is All, Skip open APs is No, and the RSSI windows are
     wide (-95 to -35 or so). Pro mode also uses its own sweep interval (8 s by default), which is slower than CAR.
   - 5 GHz channels 149-165 are scanned with the US country setting (the default). Elsewhere, build with your own
     country code (SKIRF_WIFI_COUNTRY).

F. Logs are not saved to the microSD card
   - Open Settings > Storage. A red dot means the card was not found: fit it BEFORE switching the board on, use FAT32
     (cards over 32 GB are often exFAT, which the board cannot read), try another card.
   - Tap TEST CARD: it writes, reads back and compares 64 KB, then writes again with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on, the way a
     run does. It says PASS or what failed.
   - The run report says where each log went ("... rows saved to microSD", "... to board flash", "Log NOT saved").
     The run journal (portal > Setup > Download run journal) has a line per run with any write error.
   - Logs in the board's flash still show in the Logs list (tagged FLASH) and the portal, and upload normally.
     Settings > Storage > MOVE TO CARD copies them to the card, checks every byte and only then removes them from
     flash. The board never deletes a flash log by itself.
   - KNOWN ISSUE in this beta: on some boards the card passes TEST CARD but logs written during a run do not stay on
     it. Until it is fixed, set Settings > Storage > Save logs to: FLASH.

G. I cannot reach the web portal
   - The hotspot is up for 60 seconds (at start-up, or after Settings > Web portal), and stays up while the page is
     open. The timer is on the screen.
   - Join the Wi-Fi called SKIrf_WARDRIVER (no password), then open http://192.168.4.1 (not https).
   - Some phones leave networks without internet: turn off "auto-switch to mobile data" or "Wi-Fi assist" while
     connected, or use a laptop.
   - After you start an upload from the portal the board leaves the hotspot on purpose; the phone loses the page.
   - On your home network: connect from Settings > Wi-Fi, then open the address shown there ("Portal: http://...")
     from a device on the same network, within 2 minutes. Wi-Fi stays on while the page is open.

H. Upload to WiGLE or WDGWars fails
   - The board's last upload screen and the portal's Upload tab say why:
       "Network not found" / "Password rejected" / "No answer from the router": check your home Wi-Fi (Setup tab,
         or Settings > Wi-Fi > NETWORKS) and be in range of it.
       "Can't look up ..."        the network has no internet.
       "401" / "403"              wrong key. WiGLE needs the API Name and API Token from your WiGLE account page,
                                  not your login password. WDGWars needs the API key from your profile.
       "Secure connection failed" include the message (with the memory left) in a bug report.
   - Uploads are skipped while a run is recording. Stop the run first.
   - Big files take a while. Keep the board powered and in range until it says "Upload done".

I. No sound
   The board has no speaker. Fit a PASSIVE buzzer or piezo: + to IO26, - to GND. An active buzzer plays one fixed
   pitch.

J. Reset everything
   Flash the .bin again (help/2_FLASHING.txt): this resets the settings saved in the board. Files on the microSD card
   are not touched; delete them from the Logs screen or the web portal if you want a clean card.

Still stuck? Open an issue (Bug report form) with the release from the boot screen and the Serial Monitor output.
