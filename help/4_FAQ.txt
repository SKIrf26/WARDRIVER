4. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
=============================

Does it transmit anything?
  It does not attack, jam or join other people's networks. To find networks it sends ordinary Wi-Fi probe requests
  (as every phone does), and on foot it uses active Bluetooth scanning. It runs its own open hotspot for 60 seconds
  at start-up for the web portal, and joins YOUR home Wi-Fi when you upload.

Which bands does it scan?
  Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz, and Bluetooth LE, in every sweep. The counters show each one.

What is "unique"?
  Each network or device is counted once per run. If you later pass much closer to it (10 dB stronger), it is logged
  again so WiGLE can place it better, but it is not counted twice.

What does picking a ride change?
  How often it sweeps and how long it listens on each channel:
    CAR         every 2.5 s, short dwell, passive Bluetooth, skips the slow 5 GHz radar channels on 2 of 3 sweeps
    MOTORCYCLE  every 3.5 s, a little longer dwell, balanced Bluetooth
    WALK/BIKE   every 6 s, longest dwell, active Bluetooth, every channel every sweep
  It also picks the vehicle that drives across the screen.

Why does it say WAITING FOR A GPS FIX?
  A run only scans and logs while the GPS has a fix (4 satellites, 5 on foot). This keeps every log row on the map.

What is in a log?
  A WiGLE CSV 1.6 file: one row per network or device with its address, name, security, time, channel, frequency,
  signal strength, latitude, longitude, altitude and accuracy. Files are named SKIrf_WD_C_ (car), _M_ (moto) or _W_
  (walk/bike) followed by the date and time. They upload to WiGLE as they are, and open in any spreadsheet.

Where are the logs?
  On the microSD card, in its top folder. With no card, in the board's own flash. You can see them on the Logs screen
  and in the web portal.

How do I get the files onto my computer?
  Take the card out and copy them, or use the web portal: download one file, or DOWNLOAD ALL as a .zip.

How do I upload to WiGLE?
  Make an account on wigle.net, open your Account page and copy the API Name and API Token (not your password). Save
  them in the web portal (WIGLE API KEY). Save your home Wi-Fi. Then on the board: LOGS, select runs, WIGLE.

What is WDGWars?
  A wardriving game and leaderboard at wdgwars.pl. Save your API key from your WDGWars profile in the web portal.
  LOGS > WDGW uploads runs, and Settings > WDGWars stats shows your totals.

What is Pro mode?
  An operator console for people who want to tune the scanner: Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on or off, Bluetooth duty, sweep
  interval, RSSI windows, GPS accuracy, skip open networks, de-duplication, channel plan, and a live session readout.
  It uses a plain green-on-black look. Turn it off with "Exit Pro mode".

Does it have a battery?
  No. It runs from USB power: a car USB port or a power bank works well.

Can I use the board while driving?
  Set it up and tap START before you set off, or let a passenger use it. Do not look at or touch the screen while
  driving. The STOP button needs two taps so a bump will not end your run.

How do I update?
  Flash the newer .bin over USB (help/2_FLASHING.txt).

Is it legal to use?
  Listening to openly broadcast Wi-Fi and Bluetooth is lawful in many places, but laws differ. You are responsible for
  following the laws where you are. Never join networks you are not allowed to use.

Where do I report a problem or ask for a feature?
  On the project page: Issues tab, Bug report or Feature request form.
