HELP - FIND YOUR PROBLEM FAST
=============================

I want to put the software on my board ..................... help/2_FLASHING.txt
The computer does not see the board ........................ Use a USB-C DATA cable and the LEFT USB-C port. Hold BOOT while
                                                              plugging in. (help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, A)
The screen is black or the colours are wrong ................ help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, B
Touch does nothing / lands in the wrong place ............... Settings > Touch calibration, or hold BOOT while powering on
                                                              (help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, C)
It says WAITING FOR A GPS FIX and never starts .................... help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, D
It finds few or no networks ................................. help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, E
Logs are not saved to the SD card ........................... help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, F
I cannot reach the web portal ............................... help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, G
Upload to WiGLE or WDGWars fails ............................ help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, H
No sound ..................................................... Needs a passive buzzer on IO26 and GND (help/3_TROUBLESHOOTING.txt, I)
How do I update to a newer release? ......................... Flash the new .bin again (help/2_FLASHING.txt)
Anything else ................................................ help/4_FAQ.txt and the user guide (docs folder)

If it is still stuck, open an issue on the project page (Bug report form). Include the release shown on the boot
screen (BETA 1), what you did, and what the Serial Monitor (115200 baud) printed.
